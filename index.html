THE PRODUCT

SELECTED WORKFLAGSHIP PROJECT

IDAM

Find your place.

A property discovery platform designed around the experience of finding, exploring, and connecting with properties — an India-focused, Kerala-rooted digital product where discovery, filtering, communication and property visits live in a single interaction flow.

OPEN IDAM LIVE / INDIA
THE PROBLEM
“Finding a property should feel less like searching through noise, and more like discovering the right place.”

IDAM explores a digital property experience where discovery, filtering, communication and property visits are brought into a single interaction flow — a design intent for how property search could feel when the interface does the heavy lifting.
